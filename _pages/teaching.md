In my role within Global Awareness Education at the Universität Tübingen, I bridge academic theory with interactive public policy pedagogies. I focus on developing mixed-method evaluation curricula, managing global student forums, and integrating digital civic simulation exercises.

***

### 🎓 Dynamic Course Portfolios

#### Global Challenges to Development: Introduction to SDGs (SoSe 2025)
*Graduate Teaching Assistant & Simulation Facilitator*  
* **Core Framework:** Developed instructional tracks parsing macro development dependencies, state human and development metrics, and global structural inequality points.
* **Pedagogical Innovation:** Successfully executed interactive **Graveler simulation game engine** to model policy gridlocks and strategic collaboration models in real time.

#### The Air You Breathe: Air Quality from Stuttgart to Kathmandu (WS 2025/26)
*Graduate Teaching Assistant & Institutional Context Architect*  
* **Core Framework:** Co-constructed cross-national comparative policy evaluation cases tracing legal regulatory baselines between high-resource European frameworks and municipal South Asian realities.
* **Pedagogical Innovation:** Guided students through cross-boundary policy documents and debating the outcomes of regional public health policy briefs and their effect to structural inequalities.

#### Climate Crisis: From Science to Policy on Global Nature-Based Solutions (WS 2025/26)
*Graduate Teaching Assistant & Course Logistics Coordinator*  
* **Core Framework:** Supported student evaluation groups mapping nature-based climate interventions and cases across varying administrative regimes and public funding lines.
* **Pedagogical Innovation:** Coordinated expert speaker panels and interactive classes to students of different faculty and knowledge base.
